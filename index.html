Our Mission & Philosophy Verified ISO 9001:2015 Company

Astitva Construction is an established civil contracting firm in Mumbai, India since 2009 and a Verified ISO 9001:2015 Company. Our mission is to “build quality works within set timeframe”. This is achieved through meticulous planning and thorough co-ordination with Architects. The quality can be seen physically during the progress of the work and on completion of every job.

We have a proven history of providing construction services in both new developments as well as in the repairing field. Our commitment to quality and safety during all construction activities, comprehensive schedule and cost control review procedures, and rigorous document and record keeping policies are trademarks of the company.

  • Meticulous Planning & Co-ordination: Continuous direct collaboration with architects, PMC consultants, and housing societies.
  • Strict Bar-Chart Scheduling: Systematic cash flow planning and joint physical measurements ensuring on-time delivery without cost overruns.
  • Certified Safety & Quality: Full-time on-site supervisors, accredited safety standards, and structured record keeping on every job.

Advanced Engineering

We Do Differently: Specialized Techniques

We always prefer to execute all assigned works in a professional, engineered manner rather than a haphazard one.

01 Structural Repair

Epoxy Injection

Permanent structural crack repair procedure that functions simultaneously as a structural restoration and preservation treatment.

02 Heritage Care

Historic Restoration

Specialized methods tailored to vintage concrete and masonry that vary from conventional repair to maintain architectural integrity.

03 Joint Sealing

Expansion / Control Joints

Preventing costly deterioration by identifying visible, early-warning movement signs and installing durable elastomeric sealants.

04 Leak Sealing

Urethane Injection

Injecting polyurethane to stop active water leaks, fill internal voids, provide structural support, and stabilize surrounding substrates.

05 High Pressure

Chemical Grouting

Addition of specialized additives to cementitious grouts or injection of polyurethane grouts via Highly Pressurized Grouting Machines.

06 Polymer Mortars

Concrete Resurfacing (PMM / PCM)

Polymer Modified Cement Mortar overlays to completely restore cracked, weathered, or stained concrete across Columns, Beams & RCC slabs.

07 Carbon Fiber

CRF Structural Strengthening

Carbon fiber reinforcing (CRF) is a proven structural strengthening method boasting ten times the tensile strength of conventional steel.

08 Re-strengthening

Structural Column Jacketing

Engineered jacketing treatment for severely distressed or weakened load-bearing columns to restore structural load capacity.

09 Non-Shrink Encasement

Micro Concrete Jacketing

Precision jacketing utilizing high-flow, non-shrink micro concrete for severely damaged columns and structural beams.

10 Crack-Resistant

Fibro-Elastomeric External Plaster

High-elasticity external plastering infused with fiber reinforcement to bridge structural micro-movements and stop hairline cracking.

11 BMC Standard

Sewerage & Drainage Systems

Full civil plumbing and line realignment adopting modern, durable drainage systems strictly complying with BMC guidelines.

12 Protective Finish

External Weather-Shield Painting

Approved applicator of premium Acrylic, Semi-Acrylic, Cement paint, and high-performance weather-shield protective coatings.

13 Rain Barrier

Acrylic Texture Coating

Specialized applicator of durable acrylic texture coatings providing rich architectural aesthetics and a shield against monsoon ingress.

14 Society Contracts

Annual Maintenance Service (AMS)

Structured Annual Maintenance Service (AMS) agreements for housing societies, ensuring proactive upkeep and avoiding costly major overhauls.

15 Client Support

Dedicated After-Service

Comprehensive post-completion after-care and technical assistance to clients and society committees, honoring all service commitments.

Execution Methodology

Procedure We Follow

Our proven 14-step society restoration process ensuring transparent coordination, resident comfort, and engineering excellence.

01

Erection of Scaffolding

Certified, sturdy scaffolding frameworks erected around the building perimeter with safety netting and secure ties to ensure safe working access.

02

BMC Formalities Handled

All municipal corporation (BMC) permissions, road-side safety compliances, and required statutory documentation are completely our responsibility.

03

Demarcation via Light Hammer Test

Systematic sounding inspection of all exterior walls with a light hammer test to accurately demarcate hollow, dead, and debonded plaster areas.

04

Leakage & Seepage Internal Survey

Comprehensive internal survey conducted flat-by-flat alongside the consultant and society representatives to identify root seepage points for proper treatment.

05

Standard ISI Quality Materials

We use certified standard quality materials strictly adhering to ISI specifications and tender criteria, securely stored in designated society premises.

06

On-Site Labour Camp

Organized on-site accommodation established for skilled tradesmen to avoid transit delays, eliminate labour shortages, and accelerate project execution.

07

Full-Time Site Supervisor (9 AM - 6 PM)

A qualified full-time site supervisor is stationed on site daily from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM to direct trades, maintain safety, and coordinate resident queries.

08

Consultant-Directed Structural Repairs

Structural rehabilitation, chemical injection treatments, micro-concrete encasement, and plastering carried out strictly per consultant specifications.

09

Proper Curing & Site Safety

Disciplined adherence to wet-curing cycles for civil mortars and concrete, coupled with full safety harnesses, helmets, and protective netting.

10

Society Committee Co-ordination

Continuous proactive coordination with Society Committee members and Consultants towards timely completion with minimum disturbance to residents.

11

Joint Periodic Measurements

We prefer joint measurements taken together with society representatives upon completion of each item or phase to avoid any misunderstanding.

12

100% Flat Owner Satisfaction

Complete satisfaction is our motto. We take verified satisfaction signatures from all individual flat owners after completion of work in their respective areas.

13

Systematic Scaffolding Dismantling

Orderly and safe dismantling of scaffolding frameworks following final inspection, followed by complete debris clearance and premises washdown.

14

Dedicated After-Service & AMS

Post-completion handover support, warranty certification, and Annual Maintenance Services (AMS) to maintain the restored property in peak condition.

Safety & Workforce Welfare

Health, Safety & Training Standards

We are committed to ensuring the safety of all our people, residents, and site visitors. Nothing is allowed to interfere with that overriding principle.

Health & Safety Management

Formal systems to identify risks are conducted by senior managers and external safety consultants. All management staff and operatives receive regular safety training reviewed by the concerned authority.

Certified Site Managers

All site managers are accredited with the Construction Site Manager's Safety Certificate and possess First Aid at Work qualifications, ensuring professional crisis preparedness on every site.

Safety Handbooks Issued

Every employee is issued a pocket-sized Health and Safety Handbook compiled with expert advisors — serving as a simple, handy on-site reference tool for safety protocols.

Personal Protective 'Kitbag'

All employees are issued an official kitbag containing standard industrial safety helmets, full-body safety belts/harnesses, heavy-duty gloves, high-visibility reflective vests, and ear defenders.

Continuous Staff Development

Formal appraisal systems monitor staff development. We sponsor our workforce to learn new skills and widen their technical abilities, actively recognizing hard work and commitment.

Integrity & Social Responsibility

Integrity is never compromised. Our doors are open to men and women alike, without regard to origin or personal beliefs. We seek to provide positive contributions to society and clients alike.

Society Committee Technical Consultations

Official company credentials, safety accreditations, vendor test certificates, and preliminary site inspection reports are made available to society managing committees upon request.
